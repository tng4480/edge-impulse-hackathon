# Sepsis wearable research

Use the Sunday demo to show a post-operative virtual ward catching a change worth a nurse's attention between observations. Show the UNO Q withholding an unreliable respiratory rate on a live volunteer, then switch to a labelled ICU replay for the trend model and the review handoff.

## 1. What can deteriorate at home

NICE NG253 supersedes NG51 for adults. It uses community risk criteria for suspected infection and reserves its NEWS2 recommendations for acute hospital, acute mental health and ambulance settings. Community high-risk signs include RR ≥25/min, pulse >130/min, new altered mental state on assessment, mottled or ashen skin, and no urine in 18 hours. History of new confusion or acute functional decline also matters. Ask the patient or carer about shaking chills, change from usual behaviour and last urination. The current sensors cannot assess skin appearance, mental state or urine output. In 534 selected outpatients undergoing blood cultures, reported shaking chills were 26.5% sensitive and 92.9% specific for **bacteraemia**. Acted shivering can prompt a question. A missed tap cannot assess new confusion. If a patient becomes unwell during anticancer treatment, their urgent oncology pathway takes precedence over repeat measurements. [NG51 update](https://www.nice.org.uk/guidance/ng51/update/ng51-update-1/documents/evidence-review), [NG253 community criteria](https://www.nice.org.uk/guidance/ng253/chapter/Evaluating-risk), [NICE assessment, p. 16](https://www.nice.org.uk/guidance/ng253/resources/suspected-sepsis-in-people-aged-16-or-over-recognition-assessment-and-early-management-pdf-66144015386053#page=16), [RCP NEWS2 guidance](https://www.rcp.ac.uk/media/umzn4ntq/news2_additional-guidance-002-_0.pdf), [Shaking chills study](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12237268/), [NICE CG151](https://www.nice.org.uk/guidance/cg151)

A UK virtual ward evaluation covering 1,701 matched admissions reported 92% median wearable adherence, 2.7 platform alarms per patient-day and 12% escalated to hospital. This was a single-trust retrospective evaluation without a comparator, co-authored by staff from the platform vendor's then-parent company. The count includes all platform alarms. In a separate prospective study, 35 patients continued wearing the device at home. Minute-level availability was 77% for pulse, 46% for RR and 39% for SpO₂. Coverage is worth measuring alongside accuracy. Neither study establishes home sepsis detection. [UK service evaluation](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11926843/), [At-home feasibility study](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s10877-026-01448-6)

In a Singapore Hospital at Home pilot, only eight of 248 alerts across 34 patients and 163 patient-days were clinically significant. A chest patch flagged raised RR before other signs in a patient transferred back for worsening cellulitis. Report how many alerts a nurse would review and how much usable monitoring time the motion gate costs. [Singapore pilot](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10064244/)

## 3. Model and runtime

The matched ICU window AUCs are 0.745 for LightGBM, 0.698 for the dense net and 0.642 for the four-vital NEWS2-style rule. Positive windows include post-onset hours. The 0.58 cutoff was selected from the test sweep, whose early window includes onset. I reran the counts using strictly earlier hours. At the validation-selected 0.61438 cutoff, the model caught 330/492 (67.1%) in the prior 24 hours at 0.71 alert episodes per non-sepsis patient-day. The rule caught 364/492 (74.0%) at 1.60. The exploratory 0.58 cutoff caught 358/492 (72.8%) at 0.82. The selected replay alerts 17 h before onset at the validation cutoff and 24 h at 0.58. Say which cutoff is on screen. The rule code also leaves gaps for fractional HR/RR/SpO₂ values between integer bands. Define and test the rounding rule before rebenchmarking. Keep the clocks separate. Classify 25 Hz chest motion in short windows, then resume the existing 20 to 30 s RR estimator after a clean segment. Bin fresh, distinct HR measurements and valid RR by hour. Count an SpO₂ spot once using its acquisition time, and track actual temperature acquisition time separately from packet time. Record valid coverage and elapsed gaps. Today the hourly aggregator re-adds held values, counts the current partial hour, and can hand the model mostly imputed rows. Use explicit warming-up and insufficient-data states. Keep symptom escalation separate from motion and score availability. For personalisation, start with resting HR/RR median and median absolute deviation by time of day, using clinically stable baseline days. Freeze baseline updates during illness or poor coverage. Test Raw Data → K-means or GMM on those standardized resting features, starting with one cluster/component per context. For future risk, compare an hourly discrete-time hazard model for first deterioration, with censoring at end of observation and explicit handling of competing events. ## 4. Signals we can defend

- **Chest.** Show rest, movement and RR recovery against a timed manual count. Fixed-placement 25 Hz chest sensing has distinguished upright from bed rest after lung resection. Use minutes upright or moving as exploratory context and check wear time before interpreting inactivity. Keep sleep staging and cough on the roadmap. A cough study used 1.6 kHz mechanoacoustics, and a resting SCG heart-rate study used 120 Hz. Our 40 ms sample spacing, mounting and motion artefacts make beat-to-beat SCG a poor Sunday bet. [Respiration code](../../board/sepsis-monitor/python/resp.py), [Posture study](https://doi.org/10.2490/prm.20260045), [Actigraphy vs PSG](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3792393/), [Cough study](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11653413/), [SCG study](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC9599933/)
- **Watch and room.** Five-second BPM packets cannot recover HRV. Samsung exposes quality-coded IBI in continuous HR where supported. Capture valid resting intervals and compare pulse variability against ECG. An ED study found HRV AUROC 0.754 for deterioration versus 0.775 for qSOFA, with no significant difference. Plot wrist skin temperature and separately sited Thermo air temperature. Their difference is exploratory and changes with clothing, sensor contact and ambient exposure. The Light module measures ambient RGB/IR. A refill experiment would need fixed illumination, contact pressure, timing and a clinical reference. [Samsung SDK](https://developer.samsung.com/health/sensor/guide/data-specifications.html), [ED HRV study](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/37005664/), [Thermo](https://docs.arduino.cc/resources/datasheets/ABX00103-datasheet.pdf), [Light](https://docs.arduino.cc/resources/datasheets/ABX00111-datasheet.pdf)

## 5. Product and demo

For a low-urgency signal, prompt a repeat at rest. An opted-in carer can help if the patient cannot respond. Persistent concern goes to the ward team with readings, ages, coverage and symptoms. Urgent symptoms bypass tap and repeat steps through the existing clinical pathway. Show an unsent status if connectivity drops and a contact plan for out-of-hours. The architecture supports local inference with the internet disconnected and the LAN intact. On-device execution and alert delivery still need a demo check. Its watch link uses HTTP, so keep the demo to volunteers and replay records. Clinical deployment would require an authenticated, encrypted link and an agreed retention policy. [NHS virtual ward framework](https://www.england.nhs.uk/publication/virtual-wards-operational-framework/), [Watch transport](../../wearos/README.md)

Biobeat sells a cuff-calibrated ambulatory BP patch. Current Health offers upper-arm wearable and remote monitoring workflows. Masimo SafetyNet supports home pulse oximetry with Radius PPG. Isansys PSE combines ECG-derived respiration, activity/posture, a separate thermometer and optional SpO₂ and BP. Our demo distinction is visible signal rejection feeding a local, reviewable trend alert. Indicative kit subtotal is about £380 including VAT at cited watch launch and component prices, before strap, power and logistics. Borrowing a watch reduces incremental board-plus-IMU spend to about £63. Per-patient service cost and vendor prices are unverified. Track kit reuse and nurse review minutes. ## Stop claiming or doing

- Replace “% sepsis risk” with “model score.” Drop “beats NEWS2” and any promise of 24 hours' warning at home. The comparison lacks BP, consciousness and oxygen supplementation. Display incomplete measurements explicitly. Drop “first sepsis wearable.” The PREDDICT feasibility study already investigates chest-wearable detection of deterioration, including sepsis, during CAR-T therapy. [RCP NEWS2](https://www.rcp.ac.uk/resources/national-early-warning-score-news-2/), [PREDDICT registry](https://www.hra.nhs.uk/planning-and-improving-research/application-summaries/research-summaries/pre-symptomatic-detection-of-deterioration-in-car-t-therapy-preddict/)
- Retire the fixed +3.5 °C skin-to-core estimate for live clinical scoring. Use body temperature measured with an appropriate thermometer. Wrist temperature varies even after accounting for ambient conditions. [Wrist temperature study](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8293303/) Drop automatic confusion points, continuous SpO₂ claims, rigors diagnosis and perfusion claims from ambient light. Skip new sensors and model tuning if they cut rehearsal time.
