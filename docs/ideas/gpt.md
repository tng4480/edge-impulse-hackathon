# Sepsis wearable hackathon ideas

Pitch a post-surgical virtual ward patient who deteriorates between scheduled observations. The prototype flags a change for review and shows the readings at the time of the alert. Use 4–6 hours as an example from hospital NEWS2 guidance for low scores. Home schedules vary. For chemotherapy, the existing urgent assessment pathway takes priority over any device score. [RCP monitoring guidance](https://www.rcp.ac.uk/media/mrllczrj/news2-chart-4_clinical-response-to-news-trigger-thresholds_0.pdf), [NICE neutropenic sepsis guidance](https://www.nice.org.uk/guidance/cg151/chapter/recommendations)

## Ranked build ideas

1. **Run the existing impulse on the UNO Q (2 h).** Load a local Linux `.eim` in the App Lab Python service using the Edge Impulse Linux SDK. Replay the original four channels in training order, with identical units, imputation and scaling. Verify one known window against Studio, then run without internet while preserving local watch connectivity. Show model version and measured inference time. Advance the model window one recorded hour at a time. Keep five-second watch posts on the live display. [UNO Q deployment](https://docs.edgeimpulse.com/hardware/boards/arduino-uno-q), [Linux Python SDK](https://docs.edgeimpulse.com/tools/libraries/sdks/inference/linux/python)
2. **Make motion pause the live breathing estimate (2 h).** Train Spectral Analysis + Classification on 2–4 s windows of 25 Hz chest accelerometer axes. Record `still` and `moving` in short bouts across two or three teammates, including seated and lying still, talking, turning and walking. Hold out a person or recording session before windowing. Continue the existing autocorrelation on clean segments and check one full minute against a manual breath count. When motion returns, show `Breathing rate unavailable — movement`. If training overruns, use acceleration variance and autocorrelation peak quality. For posture later, use gravity-axis means. For falls or shivers, collect controlled examples with hard negatives such as sitting abruptly or jostling the strap. Acted shivering warrants a `shiver-like motion` label. [Spectral Analysis](https://docs.edgeimpulse.com/studio/projects/processing-blocks/blocks/spectral-analysis)
3. **Stage a three-minute demo (2 h including rehearsal).** Use 30 s for the patient story, 60 s for sit–walk–sit with live RR and HR, 60 s for accelerated ICU replay and 30 s for results and limitations. Label `Live volunteer` and `Recorded ICU case · accelerated` on screen. Let judges see each hourly update, simulated spot-check markers and a timestamped `Review observations` alert. Keep a held-out non-sepsis case one click away and a recording as backup. Describe the timing as specific to the displayed case. Use browser sound or a banner if Vibro remains unavailable.
4. **Try one challenger after the demo works (60 min cap).** Train shallow XGBoost on current value, recent median, first-to-last change and slope per vital using the current and preceding hours only. Keep the patient split and compare against the dense model and four-vital NEWS2-style baseline on identical eligible windows. The supplied ROC AUCs are 0.698 and 0.642, respectively. Freeze the threshold on validation. If time permits, report held-out PR AUC, false alert episodes per patient-day, fraction of sepsis cases alerted before onset, and lead time for those cases. For cases with an observed label transition, the challenge defines onset as six hours after that transition. Export XGBoost JSON with BYOM only if the results justify a swap and the exported scores match. [Challenge definitions](https://physionet.org/content/challenge-2019/1.0.0/), [BYOM formats](https://docs.edgeimpulse.com/studio/projects/dashboard/byom)

After the deadline, try K-means on clean resting HR/RR features from each wearer’s baseline week and report deviation from baseline. For RR regression, use Raw Data + Regression on 30–60 s chest IMU windows with synchronized respiration-belt labels, then compare held-out error against autocorrelation. These are separate impulses. Use EON Tuner for a small IMU window/FFT search once the motion dataset is stable. [K-means](https://docs.edgeimpulse.com/studio/projects/learning-blocks/blocks/anomaly-detection-k-means), [Regression](https://docs.edgeimpulse.com/studio/projects/learning-blocks/blocks/regression), [EON Tuner](https://docs.edgeimpulse.com/studio/projects/eon-tuner)

## Datasets worth bookmarking

- **Early Prediction of Sepsis from Clinical Data: The PhysioNet/Computing in Cardiology Challenge 2019** (40,336 public ICU patients, open CC BY 4.0). Use tonight for matched evaluation and replay. [Dataset](https://physionet.org/content/challenge-2019/1.0.0/), [cohort size](https://physionet.org/static/published-projects/challenge-2019/1.0.0/physionet_challenge_2019_ccm_manuscript.pdf)
- **MIMIC-IV v3.1** (about 94,000 ICU stays, credentialing, training and DUA). Derive an inpatient Sepsis-3 cohort and check overlap with the challenge’s Beth Israel source before external validation. [Dataset](https://physionet.org/content/mimiciv/3.1/)
- **HiRID, a high time-resolution ICU dataset** (about 34,000 admissions, credentialing, training, DUA and project review). Benchmark circulatory deterioration. [Dataset](https://physionet.org/content/hirid/1.1.1/)
- **Auricle: A Multimodal Wearable Sensing Dataset for Cardiovascular and Hemodynamic Assessment** (70 participants, open ODC-By 1.0). Synchronized chest accelerometry and respiratory belt for RR work. [Dataset](https://physionet.org/content/auricle/1.0.0/)
- **BIDMC PPG and Respiration Dataset** (53 eight-minute recordings, open ODC-By 1.0). PPG-derived RR against annotated breaths. [Dataset](https://physionet.org/content/bidmc/1.0.0/)
- **PAMAP2 Physical Activity Monitoring** (9 participants, 18 activities, open CC BY 4.0). Use chest channels, resampled from 100 to 25 Hz, for activity classification. [Dataset](https://archive.ics.uci.edu/dataset/231/pamap2+physical+activity+monitoring)
- **PPG-DaLiA** (15 participants, open CC BY 4.0). Wrist PPG with motion and ECG reference for HR error during activity. [Dataset](https://archive.ics.uci.edu/dataset/495/ppg+dalia)
- **The COUGHVID crowdsourcing dataset: A corpus for the study of large-scale cough analysis algorithms** (v3.0, 30,000+ recordings, open download). Audio MFE + Classification for cough events with local speech and room-noise negatives. Raw chest IMU needs its own cough labels. [Dataset](https://zenodo.org/records/7024894), [Audio MFE](https://docs.edgeimpulse.com/studio/projects/processing-blocks/blocks/audio-mfe)

## Sensors and cuts

- **Tonight (30 min):** let the reviewer enter a validated upper-arm cuff reading, contact thermometer reading, carer-observed new confusion and time since last urination. Label skin temperature as skin and SpO₂ as spot. Show age since measurement, even if the watch resends a value. Mark missing or stale values. Call the comparison `Four-vital NEWS2-style baseline`. Full NEWS2 also uses systolic BP, consciousness/new confusion and oxygen supplementation. [RCP NEWS2](https://www.rcp.ac.uk/resources/national-early-warning-score-news-2/), [NICE community risk criteria](https://www.nice.org.uk/guidance/ng253/chapter/Evaluating-risk)
- **Only if already on hand:** Qwiic MAX30101 for a finger pulse waveform and exploratory perfusion trends. Motion, weak perfusion and skin pigmentation affect optical readings. Modulino Light with fixed illumination can show colour returning after fingertip blanching in a bench demo. Mottling needs wider-field imaging and validation across skin tones. Thermo gives ambient context. For a simulated catheter bag, use a NAU7802 Qwiic load cell with water, off-body. [MAX30101](https://learn.sparkfun.com/tutorials/sparkfun-photodetector-max30101-hookup-guide), [FDA oximetry limits](https://www.fda.gov/consumers/consumer-updates/pulse-oximeter-basics), [Modulino Light](https://docs.arduino.cc/hardware/modulino-light/), [NAU7802](https://docs.sparkfun.com/qwiic_nau7802_py/index.html)
- Cut cuffless BP, camera-based mottling, audio, falls and new bus debugging before sacrificing rehearsal. ICU charting, treatment and sensor differences can drive retrospective performance. Home validation needs prospective data and a measured false-alert burden.

## Top five, in order

1. Deploy the existing impulse locally and verify one replay window end to end.
2. Add motion-aware RR with a manual count and a visible unavailable state.
3. Rehearse the live-to-replay handoff and record a backup.
4. Add reading age, manual observations and a review alert with a timestamp.
5. Time-box the matched XGBoost/BYOM trial once the demo is stable.
