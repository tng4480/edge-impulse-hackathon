We built Sepsis Watch as an early-warning prototype that runs on a Galaxy Watch and a small board at home. The demo shows the board raising an alert during an ICU replay and a simulated “I feel unwell” check-in arriving in the nurse queue. In retrospective testing on held-out ICU data, the model flagged 78% of sepsis patients before onset, a median of 20 hours ahead. Home validation is still pending.
